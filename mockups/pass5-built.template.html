3. The cold open, as a film (40 s in the recording)

As storyboarded, with your change 2: the play and the omen are pure film, and the first thing the player ever does is reach for Helion. Hold, and Orbin drives into the headwind of ash (embers torn off him, his light hotter, the gap closing, a heartbeat and a swell rising with it); let go, and the tide takes him back. When their fingers touch, one clear note. Then Pull!: each tap a heave and a note of their song; three ring out, the hope chord swells, the fourth tap is a dead click. His fingers slip, "Orbin!", the shockwave, black, the three lines. Left alone four seconds, Orbin goes on by himself, so nobody can get stuck. In the recording, the scripted player lets go once during the reach, so you can see the gap open again: touch at 24.6 s, the slip at 28.7 s, the end at 40.

About the sound in the recording: the film's scored parts are the game's own file. The live sounds (heartbeat, swell, heaves, notes) are made in the browser as you play. The headless recorder can't capture those, so for the video I synthesised them at the exact moments the scripted player acted, the same way the game makes them. On your device, the game plays them itself.
