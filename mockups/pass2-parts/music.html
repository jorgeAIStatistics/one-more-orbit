All nine songs are downloaded from ncs.io and measured. Six have an instrumental on NCS and I used it. Luna, Memory Box and Darkness have no instrumental there; Darkness is sung (She Is Jules), so its voice stays unless you choose otherwise.

How they were measured. Tempo and first beat come from fitting a grid to every drum hit in the whole song. On all nine the tempo is a whole number and drifts less than 2 ms a minute, so a chart stays locked from first bar to last. Sections, drops and beat strength come from loudness and drum patterns, bar by bar. I cannot hear. Everything below is measurement; whether a song feels right for its world is yours to judge. Keys are computed estimates, and I mark the ones I am unsure of.
