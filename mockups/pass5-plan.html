1. Forgiving taps

What goes wrong today. A tap up to 0.6 beats (about 0.24 s at the Drifting Edge's tempo) before an opening or an orb crashes the run as “early”. And a tap inside the window but early makes Orbin take off early, so his arc lands off the beat.

  • The buffer. A tap up to 150 ms before an opening, a jump orb, a jump pad or a Meteor wall is held and performed exactly on time: Orbin leaves on the beat, the arc is the perfect one. It counts as “early” for timing and score (a good, not a perfect; your combo is kept), and it never kills.
  • Way too early, in the first two levels. A tap earlier than that (up to the old 0.6-beat zone) is simply ignored: nothing happens, and the opening is still waiting for your tap. Only not tapping at all fails.
  • Late taps are unchanged: inside the window they are performed at once, a little late, as now.
  • Later planets narrow it gradually, following the difficulty table:

2. Embers: checkpoints that fade as the world darkens

  • In the world. A checkpoint is a small ember lying on the course at a section line: a dark coal, until Orbin passes and it catches (a soft chime, a ring of warm light). The lit ones stay lit behind you, so you see how far you have come. No flag, no label, no box.
  • A fall takes you back to the last lit ember at once: no menu, no fade to black, just a short flash. The music rewinds one bar before the ember and plays you in: that lead-in bar plays itself (Orbin glides, nothing to tap), so the first tap you owe is the ember's own first. The planet is as it was at the ember: what you had relit stays relit.
  • The planet's star is earned by a clear with or without embers. A run with no falls earns a bonus star (see the decisions: I suggest it takes Daredevil's place).
  • Practice mode keeps an ember at every section, on every planet.

3. The cold open, as a short film (about 42 s)

In one sentence, for someone who has never played: two brothers made of light are playing in their song when a dark thing swallows the stars and drags one of them away, and the other fights to hold on and loses him.

Two places where you act, both in the struggle: hold to reach him, then tap to pull him back. Neither can fail and neither can stall: if you do nothing for four seconds, Orbin does it on his own, slower. Whatever you do, his hand slips.

0:00 – 0:08 · PLAY

Two embers. One song.

The brothers chase each other in loops through the golden nebula; their trails braid, and where they cross, a note of their song. Helion darts ahead: “Catch me!” Orbin: “Always.” (two bubbles of light; tap and Orbin catches up, a spin together). The line fades in at the top.

Sound: their duet on the music box, two voices trading phrases (Orbin a glassy bell, Helion a warmer one); a chime at every crossing; Helion's laugh is a quick bright rising run of chimes; a soft warm pad of wind under it all.

0:08 – 0:14 · THE OMEN

A note goes missing

One note of their song doesn't come: a dark hole in the braid. Helion stops and looks out. Far off, a star goes out; then a nearer one. Orbin follows his look.

Sound: the motif with one note dropped, then the loop skips like a scratched record; each star goes out with a small swallowed pluck; a low tone rises under the music; the duet stutters twice and thins to one voice.

0:14 – 0:20 · THE STRIKE

The Stillness arrives

Half a second of silence and stillness. Then it comes all at once: a vast tide of dark ink pouring in from the edge, a reaching hand within it, huge and fast, swallowing the stars as it comes. The colour drains out of the nebula in a wave; the screen shakes.

Sound: total silence for half a second (even the wind stops). Then a deep sub-bass impact, a roar of rushing ink (a reversed swell into noise), a cascade of crackles as the stars go out, and the pad bends down in pitch as the colour drains, like a tape stopping. A low rumble stays.

0:20 – 0:26 · THE REACH (you hold)

Hold on

Slow motion. The ink has Helion and drags him off his feet into the dark; he reaches back. Orbin goes after him into the Stillness's breath: a headwind of ash pouring against him. Hold and Orbin drives forward: his light burns hotter, embers are torn off him and blown back, the camera pushes in, the gap between their hands closes. Let go and the tide takes him back and the gap opens again. The ring fills while you hold; when it is full (about 2.5 s of holding), their fingertips touch.

Sound: everything slowed and muffled, as if under water. A heartbeat that speeds up while you hold; a rising string-like swell that climbs as the gap closes and sags when you let go; the howl of the headwind; the sizzle of embers torn away. When their fingers touch, one clear note.

0:26 – 0:31 · THE PULL (you tap)

Pull!

Their hands lock. Tap and Orbin heaves back with everything: a camera kick, the picture shakes, his light goes white-hot, and Helion comes a little further out of the dark, his colour coming back. Each heave rings one note of their song (the same notes as the opening's braid). After the third, Helion is half free; he looks at his brother; the song swells: it is working. The fourth tap: no note. Silence.

Sound: each tap a deep heave (a thud and a strained creak of the ink) and a note of their motif, each one brighter, the harmony filling in; after the third, the whole chord swells, hopeful. On the fourth tap, a dead click where the note should be, and everything cuts out.

0:31 – 0:35 · THE LOSS

His hand slips

The Stillness surges. In slow motion his fingers slip, one by one; Helion's last look. He is dragged into the dark calling “Orbin!”. Their song plays once more, broken. A shockwave throws Orbin away, tumbling.

Sound: a grinding low groan from the Stillness; the heartbeat stops. Helion's call is two falling notes in his bell voice (his name for his brother), under the bubble. The song once more, detuned, notes missing. Then a deep whump and a blast of white noise, and a high ringing that fades.

0:35 – 0:42 · BLACK AND SILENCE

Three lines

Black. Silence. Then, one by one: “Orbin and Helion were the two embers of the Harmonic Nebula.” / “The Stillness took Helion, and the music with him.” / “Wake the fallen stars. Bring your brother home.” Then the crash-landing, and the storyboard as now.

Sound: two seconds of true silence while the ringing dies; one low soft tone as each line appears; a hard cut into the landing.

How it is made: painted live in the game with the same brushes as these stills (the brothers rigged, the ink animated). The sound is in two parts: a scored track for the film (made by the sound tool, like the current cold open) and the struggle's sounds played live from your hold and your taps, so the swell, the heartbeat and the notes answer you. On your phone, a light buzz on every heave where the browser allows it (Android; the iPhone's browser does not, the App Store build will).
