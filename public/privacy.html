Privacy policy

ONE MORE ORBIT, website and iPhone app. Last updated 23 November 2026.

The short version

ONE MORE ORBIT does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress and settings on your own device only: best percentages, attempts, badges, the ball colour you chose, volumes, the audio and visual offsets from the sync check, and your display choices. This information never leaves your device and the developer cannot see it. Deleting the app, or clearing the website's data in your browser, erases it.

Game Center (iPhone app only)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app sends your scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The developer does not receive or store this information. You can play without Game Center, and you can sign out of it in the Settings app.

The website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details of visits, such as IP addresses, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The developer has no access to those logs. After your first visit the website keeps a copy of the game on your device so that it works offline.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
