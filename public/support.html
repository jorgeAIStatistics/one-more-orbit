Support

Help with ONE MORE ORBIT.

The taps feel early or late

Open Settings and run the sync check: tap along to eight clicks, and the game lines itself up with your speakers or headphones. Run it again whenever you change them. Bluetooth headphones add 150 to 250 ms of delay; wired headphones or the phone's speaker feel best.

There is no sound

Turn the volume up with the side buttons, and check the Music, Effects and Tap sounds sliders in Settings. In a browser, sound starts with your first tap.

How do I play?

  • Tap anywhere as the ball crosses a white mark. A little early or late still counts.
  • Gold notes are optional: they build flow and charge the drop, and can never end the run.
  • In the build-up nothing can end the run. Tap the notes to charge the drop.
  • Practice restarts from the section you reached.

Install the website as an app

On an iPhone: open the site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android or a computer: use Install in the browser's menu. After the first visit it works offline.

My progress is gone

Progress is saved on your device only. Clearing the browser's website data, or deleting the app, erases it.
